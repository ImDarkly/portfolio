- Built a 5x5 bingo generator that fills the card from eligible Minecraft items, with rarity slots so a card can lean toward common, rare or epic items.
- Made the generator backfill empty rarity slots from lower tiers, so a short item pool still produces a full card instead of a partial one; keeping the card full when a rarity pool runs dry was the hard part.
- Encoded the card into the share URL and kept card history, favourites and item settings in localStorage through the Zustand store.
